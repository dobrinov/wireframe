Structure, not style

Show what is there and how it behaves. No brand, imagery or decoration.

Greyscale only

Colour belongs to the harness: blue = agent, orange = comments.

States without colour

Ink fill = selected/done, dashed = problem, soft line = disabled/archived.

One stroke language

1 / 1.5 / 2 px lines, radii 4·6·8·10·pill, flat offset shadows only.
